3. Authenticate via Rajasthan SSO (Single Sign-On)

   - Link Aadhaar/PAN
4. Fill startup details:
   - Name: "FitNexora" or "Manglam Technical Agency"
   - Sector: AI/ML, SaaS, or Healthtech
   - Stage: Early/Revenue/Scaling
   - Founders: Vinay Pal Singh Kadela + Bhavy
5. Upload documents:
   - UDYAM certificate (RJ-15-0094091)
   - PAN card
   - Address proof
   - Brief pitch deck
6. Submit for approval (3–5 business days)

#### Step 2: Dashboard Access
Once approved:
- View startup profile
- Apply for benefits
- Track applications
- Access QRate
- Apply for incubation

## QRate Evaluation Process

### Overview
QRate is Rajasthan's official startup rating system — the only state-level rating recognized by Indian VCs. Higher QRate = higher grants + faster approvals + investor credibility.

### Prerequisites
- Registered iStart startup
- Complete business profile
- Pitch deck + business plan

### Application Steps

| Step | Action | Details |
|------|--------|---------|
| 1 | Login | iStart Dashboard |
| 2 | Navigate | Sidebar → QRate menu |
| 3 | Apply | Click "Apply for QRate" |
| 4 | Fill | Application form |
| 5 | Upload | Pitch deck, business plan, financial projections, team details |
| 6 | Submit | Wait for evaluation |
| 7 | Evaluation | iStart committee review |
| 8 | Outcome | Approved / Incomplete / Rejected |

### QRate Scoring

| Category | Score Range | Additional Criteria | Benefits |
|----------|-------------|---------------------|----------|
| Bronze | 1–50% | — | Base incentives |
| Silver | 51–70% | — | Higher grants |
| Gold | 71–85% | — | Preferred funding |
| Platinum | >85% | 50+ employees + 1 funding round | Maximum grants |
| Signature | >85% | 100+ employees + funded | Top-tier access |

**Booster Grants**: Upgrades within policy periods earn extra ₹10 lakh (e.g., 10→30 score)

### Evaluation Parameters

| Parameter | Weight | What Reviewers Look For |
|-----------|--------|------------------------|
| Idea/Innovation | High | Uniqueness, scalability, tech differentiation |
| Market Size | High | TAM, SAM, SOM analysis |
| Team | High | Founder experience, execution capability |
| Revenue Model | Medium | Sustainability, unit economics |
| Product Readiness | Medium | MVP, traction, user metrics |
| Investment Worthiness | Medium | Growth potential, exit strategy |

**MTA Advantage**: Your 10-stage pipeline documentation already exceeds most startups' process maturity.
