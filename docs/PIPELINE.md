# MTA 10-Stage Client Pipeline Guide

> Complete workflow documentation for Manglam Technical Agency client engagements.

---

## Executive Summary

MTA's 10-stage client pipeline is **more structured and protective** than 90% of Jaipur agencies and many international boutiques. This document details:
- Each stage's purpose, deliverables, and success criteria
- International benchmarks (US, UK, Europe)
- Tool recommendations
- Implementation templates

**MTA Advantage**: Founder-direct access + paid discovery workshop + full documentation + 1-year maintenance + DPDP/GST compliance.

## The 10-Stage Pipeline

### Stage 1: Lead Generation & First Contact
**Purpose**: Attract qualified prospects and establish initial connection

| Aspect | Details |
|--------|---------|
| **Inputs** | Website inquiries, referrals, iStart network, LinkedIn outreach |
| **Outputs** | Qualified lead with basic requirements |
| **Owner** | Founder (Vinay) |
| **Tools** | HubSpot CRM, WhatsApp Business |

**Activities**:
- Respond to website contact forms (2–4 hour SLA)
- LinkedIn engagement and content sharing
- iStart ecosystem referrals
- Word-of-mouth from existing clients

**Success Metrics**:
- Response time < 4 hours
- Lead quality score (budget/timeline/service fit)
- Conversion to discovery call > 40%

**Global Comparison**:
- Jaipur typical: Free consult immediately
- International: Content-led inbound + paid audits
- **MTA**: Direct founder response + NDA gate

## International Benchmarks

### Directive Consulting (US)
- **DiscoverabilityOS**: Full-funnel (awareness → pipeline → revenue)
- **Discovery**: Paid deep audit first
- **Delivery**: Performance/creative/revenue ops execution
- **Focus**: Pipeline velocity over MQLs

### Refine Labs (US)
- **Investigation Phase**: 4–8 quarters revenue/funnel data analysis
- **Strategy**: Custom demand strategy only after deep diagnostics
- **Results**: 95% clients hit 50%+ qualified pipeline growth

### SmartBug Media (US)
- **30-Day Onboarding**: Structured with goals/KPIs worksheet
- **Playbook**: Intelligent Inbound methodology
- **Transparency**: Clear timelines and deliverables

### WebFX (US)
- **7–8 Steps**: Contract → Goals → Account manager → Analytics → Kickoff → Updates → Results
- **Focus**: Revenue acceleration via proprietary tools
- **Scale**: Large team, dedicated resources

### KlientBoost (US)
- **Free Marketing Plan** → Deep audit → Revenue-ops integration
- **Metrics**: MQL/SQL/pipeline/revenue targets (not vanity)
- **Results**: 63% avg ROI lift in first 3 months
